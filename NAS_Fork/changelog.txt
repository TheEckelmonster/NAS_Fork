Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-04-27
  Features:
    - Updated graphic (setting available for the previous graphic if desired)
    - Added several configurable settings for the Nuclear artillery shell item, recipe, and technology
      - Configurable settings for:
        - Weight modifier
        - Stack size
        - Crafting time
        - Recipe (ingredients/inputs)
        - Results (output(s))
        - (Default) crafting machine
        - Additional crafting machines
        - Emissions multiplier
        - Research prerequisites
        - Research ingredients
        - Research time
        - Research count
    - Changed the default technology requirements: (can be modified with the above settings)
      - Research count: 1000 -> 5000
      - Research time:  15s  -> 60s
      - Research prerequisites: (added science packs required for artillery and atomic-bombs in each context)
        - [Vanilla] added:
          - Production-science-pack
        - [Space-Age] added:
          - Metallurgic-science-pack & space-science-pack
        - [Space-Exploration] added:
          - Space-science-pack & production-science-pack
          - Removed utility-science-pack
  Compatibility:
    - Added Space-Exploration as an optional dependency
